O R D E R

Application seeking condonation of delay in filing Review Petition is allowed.

The concurrent view taken by the Sessions Court and the High Court in convicting and sentencing
the petitioner for having committed offence punishable under Section 302 read with Section 34 of
the Indian Penal Code was under challenge before this Court. Having considered the matter, this
Court found no reasons to interfere and the Special Leave Petition was dismissed.

The grounds taken in Review Petition do not make out any error apparent on record to justify
interference.

This Review Petition is, therefore, dismissed.
