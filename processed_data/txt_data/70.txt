20. To our mind, the prosecution has succeeded in proving its case beyond reasonable doubt.
Having carefully gone through the material on record, especially the depositions of the witnesses
and upon a keen examination of the relevant aspects of the case, we find that the presence of the
appellant at the site of the incident and him having stabbed the deceased on the stomach repeatedly
